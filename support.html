Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas? Check the answers below or send a message — you'll get a reply by email.

How do I add a book?

In the library tap + (or Add an ebook) and pick an EPUB, PDF or TXT file from the Files app (or any connected cloud drive). Free public-domain books are available at gutenberg.org.

My PDF shows no text.

Scanned PDFs contain only images of pages, so there is no text to read. Try an EPUB or a text-based PDF of the same book.

How do I change the speed?

In Settings you can set your top speed (200–1000 words per minute), your starting speed and how quickly each session ramps up.

How do I go back or jump to a different spot?

Tap the word to pause. While paused you see the surrounding text — tap any word to continue from there, or use the buttons to go back to the start of the sentence or skip to the next one.
