Read faster without losing the thread.

EasyRead is a speed reader for iPhone and Android that shows one word at a time. The focus letter of every word stays in the same place, and each word's timing adapts to punctuation, word length, paragraphs and dialogue, so you can read faster and still understand the story.

Import your EPUB, PDF and TXT books, set your speed, and build a daily reading habit. No account, no ads, no data collected.
