Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

EasyRead does not collect, store or share any personal data.

Your books and reading data

Books you import, your reading positions, settings, daily goal and reading streak are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

EasyRead has no sign-up, shows no ads, and contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software. It does not use the advertising identifier and does not track you across apps or websites.

Notifications

Daily reading reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No server is involved.

Permissions

EasyRead only reads files you explicitly choose to import. It asks for notification permission only if you turn on reminders. On Android it may ask for Do Not Disturb access, used only to silence interruptions while you read, if you enable that option.

Contacting support

If you use the support form, the email address and message you enter are delivered to the developer's mailbox and used only to answer you. They are not used for any other purpose and you can ask for them to be deleted at any time.

Children

EasyRead does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the support form.
